First beta of LocSim.

Please test:
- Setup: import the pairing file, connect LocalDevVPN, set a first location on Wi-Fi.
- Setting locations: long-press the map, search for a place, enter coordinates
  (decimal or degrees/minutes/seconds), use favorites.
- Keeping it: leave the app, lock the device, switch to cellular. The location
  should stay simulated until you tap Clear.
- Clear and Stop and Quit: the real location must come back.
- After a device restart: setting a location must work again.

If something fails, tap the status line, choose Copy Details and send it with
your feedback.
