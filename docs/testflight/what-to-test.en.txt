Build 5: widgets, Control Center, quick actions, landscape and iPhone Duo, a new Shortcuts action and fixes from a review round.

Please test:
- Home Screen widget: add the Location Status widget. It shows whether a location is simulated and where; the medium size also lists the three most used favorites. Tapping one opens LocSim and sets it.
- Lock Screen widgets: inline, rectangular and circular. When LocSim may have ended in the background, they say "Open LocSim to check the location".
- Control Center (iOS 18 or later): add Set Last Location and Clear Location. Set Last Location opens LocSim, Clear Location works without opening it.
- Quick actions: hold the LocSim icon. While a location is simulated it offers Clear Location, otherwise Set Last Location, then the two most used favorites and Search. Most used means runs of 30 minutes or more, then total time, counted on this device only.
- Landscape: turn the iPhone. Search runs across the top, the panel sits at the bottom right. On the iPhone Duo, check the cover screen and, unfolded, the sidebar next to the map.
- Shortcuts: the new action Set Location to Place takes a name, an address, coordinates or a map link.
- Reset by iOS: if iOS goes back to the real location while LocSim runs, LocSim should send the simulated one again right away. Needs location access.
- Restart: after closing and reopening LocSim, Set Last Location keeps the place name.
- VoiceOver: coordinates are read in words, also in the widgets and the Live Activity.

Connecting still needs Wi-Fi; once connected, cellular works.

If something fails, tap the status line, choose Copy Details and send it with your feedback.
