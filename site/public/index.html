LocSim does not open right now

Apple has ended all TestFlight builds. On 13 November Apple named the reason: an automated check flagged the local tunnel LocSim uses as a VPN and asked what data it collects. LocSim collects none, the tunnel only reaches the device itself. I have answered, and LocSim is back in review. Until then the installed beta does not open. The source code stays fully available on GitHub, and new features stay paused until the review is through. Your favorites and settings stay on your device. If a simulated location is still shown, restart the device.

Widgets

Location Status shows on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen whether a location is simulated and where. The medium size also lists up to three favorites, the most used or ones you choose; a tap sets one in LocSim.

Control Center and app icon

Set Last Location and Clear Location as controls on iOS 18 or later. Hold the app icon for Clear Location or Set Last Location, your two most used favorites and Search.

Shortcuts

Set Location to Place takes a place name, an address, coordinates or a map link. Other actions set a favorite or clear the location, also with Siri. Automations can check whether a location is simulated and which one.

    Tap Clear or close LocSim and the real location returns.

Questions

Is LocSim free?

Yes. LocSim is free and open source under the MIT license.

Does it need a jailbreak?

No. LocSim uses developer features that iOS provides once Developer Mode is enabled.

Where can I get it?

Through the public beta or Early Access on TestFlight, or by building it yourself with Xcode.

Why does iOS show a VPN?

LocSim reaches the device's own developer services through a local tunnel, which iOS lists as a VPN configuration. The tunnel routes only the address 10.7.0.1 and sends that traffic back to the device. It stops with LocSim, or by itself about two minutes after LocSim was closed. Other traffic is not affected, nothing goes to a server and no data is collected.
