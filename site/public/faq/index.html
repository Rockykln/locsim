Frequently asked questions

Short answers about LocSim. Error codes are listed on the support page.

About LocSim

Is LocSim free?

Yes. LocSim is free and open source under the MIT license.

Does it need a jailbreak?

No. LocSim uses developer features that iOS provides once Developer Mode is enabled.

Where can I get it?

Through the public beta or Early Access on TestFlight, or by building it yourself with Xcode.

Why does iOS show a VPN?

LocSim reaches the device's own developer services through a local tunnel, which iOS lists as a VPN configuration. The tunnel routes only the address 10.7.0.1 and sends that traffic back to the device. It stops with LocSim, or by itself about two minutes after LocSim was closed. Other traffic is not affected, nothing goes to a server and no data is collected.

Using LocSim

The location jumps back to the real one

iOS resets the simulated location when LocSim is closed. Leave the app running in the background, with location access While Using the App and Keep Alive in Background on. If iOS resets it while LocSim is running, LocSim sends it again as soon as it notices.

Other devices show the real location

LocSim changes the location of the iPhone it runs on. An iPad, a Mac or AirPods on the same Apple Account get their location on their own, for example over Wi-Fi, and Find My and location sharing may report the location of one of them. If the iPhone itself returns to the real location while another device is nearby, LocSim sends the simulated one again as soon as it notices. For testing, turn off Location Services on the other device.

Connecting fails on cellular

Building a connection needs Wi-Fi, any network without internet is enough. Once connected, locations can be changed on cellular. After Clear or closing LocSim, the next connection needs Wi-Fi again.

It stopped working after an iOS update

Pairing files can expire with an update, a reset or after clearing trusted computers. Create a new one, see the setup guide.

Can I use LocSim from widgets or Shortcuts?

Yes. The Location Status widget shows on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen whether a location is simulated; the medium size also lists up to three favorites, the most used or ones you choose, and a tap sets one in LocSim. If LocSim may have ended without saying so, the widget asks you to open LocSim to check. On iOS 18 or later, Control Center offers Set Last Location and Clear Location. Holding the app icon shows Clear Location while a location is simulated (otherwise Set Last Location), the two most used favorites and Search. Shortcuts has Set Favorite, also by Siri phrase such as “Set LocSim to Office”, Set Coordinates, Set Location to Place for a name, address, coordinates or map link, and Clear Location. Setting a location opens LocSim; Clear Location in Control Center or Shortcuts works without opening it. Most used means how often a favorite stayed simulated for 30 minutes or more, then the total time. It is counted only on the device.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17.4 or later, in portrait and landscape. On the iPhone Duo, LocSim also uses the cover screen and, unfolded, a layout with a sidebar.

Do I need a computer?

Once for the setup, and again whenever a new pairing file is needed, for example after an iOS update.

Is any data sent to you?

LocSim itself sends nothing to us. If you are a TestFlight tester, Apple shares crash reports and feedback with us. Details are in the privacy policy.

How do I remove everything again?

Delete LocSim, delete the pairing file from your computer, and open Settings > Developer > Clear Trusted Computers to revoke the pairing. Optionally turn off Developer Mode and turn off Show this iPhone when on Wi-Fi.
