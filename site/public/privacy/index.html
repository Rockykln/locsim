Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

The LocSim app collects no personal data and sends nothing to us. We only process data that you send us by email or that Apple provides to us through TestFlight. LocSim has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

The app on your device

  • Pairing file: stored in the app's private storage, protected by iOS data protection and excluded from backups. Removed in Settings or when you delete the app.
  • Favorites and settings: stored on the device and included in its backups.
  • Log: kept in memory only and cleared when the app quits. It leaves the device only if you copy it yourself.
  • Location: your real location is used on the device to keep the connection open in the background and, after you tap the locate button, to show it on the map. LocSim does not send it anywhere.

Connections the app makes

These connections go directly from your device to the respective provider. We have no access to them. The providers process the data as separate controllers under their own privacy policies.

  • Your device itself, through LocalDevVPN, to simulate the location.
  • Apple Maps for map tiles and place search. Apple receives search terms, the visible map area and your IP address. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), only if the Developer Disk Image has to be downloaded. GitHub receives your IP address.
  • Apple's signing server, only when the Developer Disk Image is installed. Apple receives the device identifiers needed to personalize it.

TestFlight

To invite you, your email address and, if you provide it, your name are entered in Apple's App Store Connect. During the beta, Apple collects crash reports, feedback, device information and usage data and makes them available to us, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (your participation in the beta). The data is kept until you leave the beta or it ends.

Email

Emails to locsim@rockykln.com are received through Cloudflare Email Routing and forwarded to an iCloud Mail mailbox (Apple). Your address and message are used only to answer you. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for beta requests, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our interest in answering enquiries). Emails are deleted once the matter is closed, unless statutory retention applies.

This website

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. To deliver it and protect it from abuse, Cloudflare processes your IP address and request data on our behalf. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (secure and reliable delivery). The data is kept according to Cloudflare's retention periods. The site sets no cookies and loads no analytics or external resources.

If you change the theme, your choice is stored in your browser under the key locsim-theme (auto, light or dark). It is never transmitted and stays until you clear it. This is strictly necessary for the function you requested (§ 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG), so no consent is required.

The website button in LocSim opens locsim-app.rockykln.com, which only redirects to this site. Cloudflare counts requests to it in aggregate; we store nothing about you.

Recipients and transfers

Recipients are Cloudflare, Inc. (USA), Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland, and Apple Inc., USA) and GitHub, Inc. (USA). Transfers to the USA are based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or on the EU standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability (Art. 15 to 18 and 20 GDPR). You can object at any time to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21 GDPR). Write to locsim@rockykln.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for example the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen.

Providing data is voluntary. Without an email address we cannot send a TestFlight invite. There is no automated decision-making.
